AGT-01 In delivery

AI Readiness Assessment

The diagnostic I run before anyone touches a rollout.

The job

CIOs and CTOs want to scale AI but can't tell you whether their org is actually ready. This agent runs a structured assessment across five domains — data, workflow, governance, talent, and vendor maturity — and scores each one.

How it works

A guided intake walks the stakeholder through each domain, then produces two deliverables from one pass: a scored maturity breakdown for practitioners and an executive risk brief written for the board.

Where I stay in the loop

The agent structures and scores; I set the thresholds and own the risk calls. A readiness number nobody will defend in a budget meeting is worthless — so the judgment stays mine.

AGT-02 In delivery

AI Adoption Blockers Diagnostic

For the org that bought the tool and watched no one use it.

The job

The most common AI failure isn't technical — it's a tool that's live and idle. This agent runs a post-deployment diagnostic when the pilot shipped and adoption flatlined.

How it works

A five-phase intake covers deployment context, resistance sources, training, leadership sponsorship, and workflow integration — then returns a ranked blocker list with root-cause hypotheses and three prioritized interventions.

Where I stay in the loop

It's a change-management problem wearing a technology costume. The agent surfaces the pattern; I decide which blocker is the real one and which intervention an actual organization will tolerate.

AGT-03 Adversarial

The Skeptical CIO

An agent built to attack my own thinking before a reader does.

The job

Before I publish a thesis or hand a client a recommendation, I need it broken. This agent plays a skeptical CIO and stress-tests the argument — hunting weak claims, pattern-without-proof assertions, and anything a smart critic would puncture.

How it works

It reads a near-final draft adversarially and returns the three strongest counterarguments a real decision-maker would raise, plus the specific sentences that won't survive scrutiny.

Where I stay in the loop

This is the clearest expression of my rule: AI is fastest as a critic of my expertise, not a substitute for it. It finds the holes; I decide which ones are fatal and rewrite from experience, not from its suggestions.

AGT-04 In delivery

Content Ops Diagnostic

Turns "our docs are a mess" into a scored, fundable liability.

The job

Enterprise content operations fail quietly across tooling, governance, ownership, workflow, and findability. This agent runs a multi-domain health check and scores each domain on a 0–4 maturity scale.

How it works

It produces a technical-debt breakdown for the practitioners doing the work and a remediation roadmap with business-impact framing for the executive who controls the budget — one diagnosis, two altitudes.

Where I stay in the loop

The scores are only useful once they're translated into a story leadership will fund. The agent quantifies; I frame the business case and stake my name on the prioritization.

AGT-05 Persistent

Client Knowledge Graph

Solves the "getting back up to speed" tax of fractional work.

The job

Running several fractional engagements means constant context-switching. This agent captures organizational context — from calls, notes, and docs — into a persistent knowledge graph for each client.

How it works

It structures raw intel into people, systems, and relationships, then generates a re-entry brief before a call and a clean handoff artifact when a contract ends or converts to a full-time role.

Where I stay in the loop

The graph is memory, not judgment. It remembers what was said; I decide what it means and what to do next. The relationships it can't see are exactly the ones that matter — so I read every brief before I walk in.

AGT-06 Voice-guarded

Voice Editor

Drafts in my voice without letting the AI think for me.

The job

I publish regularly for CIO and CTO audiences. This agent helps me draft and edit faster — while refusing to flatten my writing into the generic, hedged register that gives AI-written content away.

How it works

It's tuned on my actual voice — pattern-recognition-forward, zero vendor theater, confrontational where it earns it — and edits toward that register instead of away from it. It handles structure and mechanics so I can spend my time on the argument.

Where I stay in the loop

Every claim, every pattern, every opinion originates with me. The agent shapes the sentences; it never sources the insight. If a paragraph says something I couldn't defend in a room, it doesn't ship — no matter how well it reads.

The through-line

One rule runs through all of them

Every agent I build has a human-in-the-loop seam — an explicit point where my judgment enters and the AI's stops. That seam isn't a limitation. It's the product.

On agent design

An agent that produces a confident answer nobody will defend is worse than no agent at all. Speed without accountability is just faster liability.

On why the loop matters

I don't sell the agents. I sell the diagnosis they help me deliver — and the expertise that decides which of their outputs is true.
