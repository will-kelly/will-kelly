Every long-form piece earns its derivatives

A piece isn't finished when it publishes. It's finished when its derivatives ship. Every newsletter post generates a LinkedIn post and a follow-up comment thread. Every how-to generates an observation post built from its strongest single pattern. Every product launch generates an announcement and a newsletter mention. The repurposing map is part of the pipeline, not an afterthought.

What the writing covers

  • Enterprise AI: readiness, governance, ROI, hallucination management, and the business case behind adoption decisions.
  • Content operations and documentation debt: maturity models, governance, knowledge management, and what dysfunction actually costs.
  • Cloud and FinOps: cost optimization, cloud-smart strategy, and Kubernetes cost management.
  • DevOps and platform engineering: CI/CD pipelines, developer experience, and container workloads.
  • Federal and public-sector IT: drawn from GDIT, GSA, and systems integrator experience.

What it doesn't cover: consumer technology, vendor cheerleading, and "AI will replace your job" takes with no organizational context.
